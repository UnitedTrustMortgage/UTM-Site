Our Loan Options

Not sure which program fits? Our loan officers will walk you through every option and find the perfect match for your goals.

🏡

Conventional

The gold standard of home financing. Competitive rates, flexible terms, and down payment options as low as 3% for qualified borrowers. Ideal for buyers with strong credit and documented income.

Agency
💎

Jumbo

Financing above conforming loan limits for high-value properties. Competitive rates on loan amounts starting at $766,550+ with flexible qualifying criteria for high-net-worth borrowers.

Agency
🇺🇸

VA Loans

Exclusive benefits for veterans, active-duty service members, and eligible surviving spouses. No down payment, no PMI, and competitive rates — a thank-you for your service.

Government
🏦

FHA Loans

Government-backed financing with down payments as low as 3.5% and more lenient credit requirements. An excellent path to homeownership for first-time buyers and those rebuilding credit.

Government
📊

Bank Statement

Self-employed borrowers qualify using 12–24 months of personal or business bank statements instead of tax returns. No W-2s needed — ideal for business owners and freelancers.

NonQM
📈

P&L Loans

Qualify using a CPA-prepared profit & loss statement — no bank statements required. Designed for business owners with complex finances who show strong business performance.

NonQM
🗣️

Stated Income

Asset-based qualification using stated income and verified assets. Perfect for high-net-worth individuals, retirees, and investors whose tax returns don't reflect their true financial picture.

NonQM
🏘️

DSCR

Debt Service Coverage Ratio loans let investment properties qualify based on rental income — not personal income. Ideal for real estate investors scaling a portfolio without income documentation.

NonQM
📋

Alt-Doc

Alternative documentation loans for borrowers who don't fit conventional molds. From 1099-only qualification to asset depletion — we find the documentation path that works for you.

NonQM
🔨

Construction

Finance the build of your custom home with a single-close construction-to-permanent loan. One application, one closing, one rate — from groundbreak to move-in day.

Specialty
🔧

Fix & Flip

Short-term bridge financing for real estate investors acquiring, renovating, and reselling properties. Fast funding, flexible terms, and draw schedules aligned with your project timeline.

Specialty
🤝

Not Sure Which Fits?

Talk to a loan officer. We'll match you with the right program in minutes — no obligation, no pressure.

About Arin

A Specialist When
It Matters Most

Arin Baghermian is a NonQM underwriter, trainer, and mortgage broker with deep expertise in alternative documentation loan programs. He serves as a loan officer at United Trust Mortgage and works across Southern California helping self-employed borrowers, real estate investors, and clients with complex financial profiles secure the financing they need.

With hands-on experience underwriting and originating DSCR, Bank Statement, P&L, and Alt-Doc loans, Arin brings a rare combination of technical depth and deal-closing ability to every transaction. If conventional lenders have turned you away, Arin knows how to find a path forward.

He is known for his responsiveness, transparency, and ability to move quickly — because in real estate, timing is everything.
