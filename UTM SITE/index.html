About Arin

A specialist when
it matters most.

Arin Baghermian is a Non-QM underwriter, trainer, and mortgage broker with deep expertise in alternative documentation loan programs. He serves as a loan officer at United Trust Mortgage and works across Southern California helping self-employed borrowers, real estate investors, and clients with complex financial profiles secure the financing they need.

With hands-on experience underwriting and originating DSCR, bank statement, P&L, and alt-doc loans, Arin brings a rare combination of technical depth and deal-closing ability to every transaction. If conventional lenders have turned you away, Arin knows how to find a path forward.

He is known for his responsiveness, transparency, and ability to move quickly — because in real estate, timing is everything.
